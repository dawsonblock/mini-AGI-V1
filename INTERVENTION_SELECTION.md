# Campaign 3B.3 — evidence-driven intervention selection (v2)

Status: **executed under the corrected oracle.** Generated by
`scripts/analysis/intervention_selection.py`; machine-readable result in
`INTERVENTION_SELECTION.json`. Supersedes the v1 selection record —
the earlier version measured casefold-era effects and converted a
confounded rank sweep into a categorical refusal.

## Decision taxonomy

The selector distinguishes evidence states, not just outcomes:

| state | meaning |
|---|---|
| `RECOMMENDED` | targets the largest lever measured beyond seed noise |
| `ELIGIBLE` | measured, not contradicted, not dominant |
| `ELIGIBLE-UNMEASURED` | lever never measured; ranked on priors only |
| `HIGH-RISK` | benefit measured but the mechanism's record destroys capability |
| `CONFOUNDED` | the measurement cannot isolate the lever from a covariate |
| `INCONCLUSIVE` | spread does not exceed measured seed noise |
| `INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE` | too few observations to measure |
| `FAILED-IMPLEMENTATION` | the mechanism's only trials failed implementation gates |
| `REFUTED` | measured direction contradicts the claim *beyond noise* — the only true refusal |

`CONFOUNDED`, `INCONCLUSIVE` and `INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE` keep a
research direction open at low priority — they never become refusals.

## The corrected E5 decision

`E5_rank_or_target_modules` → **CONFOUNDED**, priority low.

The v1 selector refused it: the ladder was non-monotone and the spread
marginally exceeded seed noise. The corrected read: the sweep changed
`alpha/r` alongside rank, so it measured capacity × update-scale
jointly — it cannot isolate either factor, and a confounded experiment
is not evidence *against* a mechanism. Preserved directions: rank
allocation at fixed scaling, alpha/r sweeps, dynamic LoRA.

## The E2 recommendation, precisely

`E2_replay_during_training` → **RECOMMENDED**, and it is not the L7
mechanism: L7 injected replayed examples into the *prompt at inference
time* over adapted weights (hidden 0.083, destroyed 4/4 frozen-correct).
E2 interleaves verified-correct pairs into the *gradient stream* on a
deterministic schedule with the same response-only loss — gradient mass
on behavior the frozen model already has, directly opposing the
measured destruction. P2/P3 implement it in `peft_trainer.py`;
P4 measures it in `results/campaign3b3/`.

## Post-P4 correction (executed evidence)

The P4 matched multi-seed sweep measured this recommendation directly
(`results/campaign3b3/MULTISEED_COMPARISON.json`): rehearsal-in-
gradients **underperformed** — nets [0, −1, −1], adapted hidden 0.278
*below* frozen 0.333. KL was neutral [+1, −1, 0]; rehearsal+KL was
[+1, −1, +1] (p = 1.0). No adaptation condition beat frozen.

This record stands: the selector recommended correctly *from the
evidence available* (preservation was the dominant measured harm),
and the experiment then measured the remedy and found it wanting at
this scale. That is exactly what an evidence-driven controller is
for — the selection record is retained unchanged, and the outcome
is now part of the evidence base the next selection must read.
