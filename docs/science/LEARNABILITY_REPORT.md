## Interpretation

- **The repaired pipeline works.** Parity and supervision gates pass on
  real data; the receipt records the contract digests; the adapter
  reloads bit-identically; the negative control collapses (0.067 train)
  while correct supervision learns — the signal is not spurious.
- **LoRA learns within-family** (validation 0.222 → 0.444, double the
  frozen baseline) but does **not** fit the training set to the 95% bar
  (0.767) and **degrades** the held-out families (0.167 vs 0.417
  frozen). At 30 examples / 60 steps this is consistent with
  overfitting rather than transfer.
- **The verified replay baseline operates** (30/30 records admitted,
  20/20 queries hit, 760 injected tokens — Campaign 3A had zero traces)
  and is the strongest hidden-block arm. The self-verified L5a stayed
  empty, as in 3A — preserved as the honest negative result.
- **L5b is not in the readiness table**: the report tool measures the
  neural arms; L5b's performance is recorded in the campaign
  `RESULT.json` above.
